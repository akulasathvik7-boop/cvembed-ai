JOB DESCRIPTION: SENIOR UI/UX DESIGNER
Company: DesignLabs
Location: New York / Remote

ABOUT THE ROLE:
We are looking for a Senior UI/UX Designer to lead the design of our next-generation project management tool. You will work closely with product managers and engineers to define the visual language and user experience of our platform.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Conduct user research and translate insights into wireframes and high-fidelity designs.
- Maintain and expand our Figma design system.
- Create interactive prototypes for stakeholder review.
- Ensure consistent UI across web and mobile platforms.

QUALIFICATIONS:
- 4+ years of experience in Product Design or UI/UX Design.
- Proficiency in Figma and Adobe Creative Suite.
- Strong understanding of typography, color theory, and layout.
- Experience with user research methodologies.
- Portfolio demonstrating your design process and finished products.
